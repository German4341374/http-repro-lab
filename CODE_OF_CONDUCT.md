# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community. Be constructive, assume good faith, respect privacy, and focus criticism on observable behavior and code. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of another person's confidential data are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact mechanism. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation to protect the community.
